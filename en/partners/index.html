How it works

We produce professional live streams of action sports events. The brand pays for the production, the organiser gets the stream for free, and the brand gets the stage, the reach and all the content. At the brand's own event, we produce the complete broadcast on the brand's own channel.

Frequently asked questions

Who organises the event?

The organiser. We handle the complete live production; the brand doesn't need to organise anything.

Which channel does the stream go out on?

On the organiser's channel, or with the Brand channel package on the brand's own channel.

What if there's no internet at the venue?

Then we bring our own Starlink.

Can we start small?

Yes. We like to start with one event as a pilot and then review the report together.
