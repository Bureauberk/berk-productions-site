Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This privacy policy explains which personal data BERK Productions processes, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are. We collect no more than we need and never sell data to anyone.

Who we are

BERK Productions is a trade name of Bureau BERK, a sole proprietorship based in Apeldoorn, the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 95395792. Bureau BERK is the controller of the data described in this policy.

Questions about privacy can be sent to wouter@berk.productions or asked on +31 6 55 76 46 78.

What data we process and why

Contact form, quote request and price calculator

When you fill in a form, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, organisation, the name and date of your event, and if you provide them your phone number, place and message. The price calculator also sends the production you put together, and every form sends the page it was submitted from.

We use this data to answer your question and prepare a quote. The legal basis is that you ask us to take steps before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Forms are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a service that forwards the message to our mailbox.

Email and phone

If you email or call us, we process your contact details and what you tell us in order to help you. The legal basis is preparing a contract or our legitimate interest in answering questions.

Clients

If you become a client, we process the data needed to carry out the production and invoice it, such as contact person, billing address and payment details. The legal basis is performance of the contract and our legal accounting obligations.

Visiting the website

Our website is hosted by Vercel. On every visit Vercel processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and time of visit, to run and secure the site. This is our legitimate interest.

The fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser fetches them from Google, which receives your IP address. This is also a legitimate interest: a readable, fast-loading site.

Microsoft Clarity, only with your consent

If you accept in the cookie banner, we use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use the site. Clarity records clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, makes session recordings and heatmaps, and processes data about your device, browser and approximate location. Clarity masks text you type into forms by default. According to Microsoft, session recordings are kept for 30 days and aggregated data such as heatmaps for up to 13 months.

Clarity sets cookies for this, including Clarity's own _clck and _clsk and Microsoft cookies such as MUID. They last up to a year. Without your consent we don't load Clarity and these cookies are not set. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time through the cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

The website itself sets no cookies. We only remember your choice about Clarity, in your own browser's storage.

Videos from YouTube

Our partner pages contain a YouTube video. It only loads when you click play, via youtube-nocookie.com. Only then does Google (YouTube) receive data such as your IP address, and YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies. If you don't click, nothing is sent to YouTube.

Productions

At live streams and recordings we film on behalf of the event organiser. The organiser informs participants and spectators that the event is filmed and broadcast.

Who we share data with

We only share data with parties we need to deliver our service:

  • Vercel, for hosting the website.
  • FormSubmit, for forwarding forms.
  • Our email provider, for receiving and sending email.
  • Google, for the fonts and, if you play a video, YouTube.
  • Microsoft, for Clarity, only with your consent.
  • Our accountant and the Dutch tax authorities, for client accounting.

Some of these parties are based in the United States. Data only leaves the European Economic Area with appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Requests and messages that do not lead to an assignment: up to two years.
  • Client data: seven years, because of Dutch tax retention rules.
  • Clarity data: as described above, according to Microsoft's retention periods.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, restrict its use and have it transferred. If you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Send your request to wouter@berk.productions. We respond within one month.

If you disagree with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the supervisory authority in your own country.

Security

The website runs entirely over an encrypted connection (https). Access to the mailbox and the accounts is limited to the people who need it. We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Changes

We update this privacy policy when our way of working or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest update.
