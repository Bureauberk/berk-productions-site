Good to know

Why we don’t make aftermovies

An aftermovie needs a completely different way of filming. A filmmaker roams freely all day with their own camera and their own plan: atmosphere, details, the crowd, the people behind the scenes. That kind of footage doesn’t come out of a control room.

We point all our cameras at the competition and the broadcast. From that footage we make highlights and clips per athlete: fast, honest and ready to use for your channels and your sponsors.

Want an aftermovie as well? We’re happy to recommend a filmmaker who specialises in them, and we’ll coordinate with them on the day.
